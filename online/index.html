Who am I and Why me?

Hi! I'm Adaobi.

You can also call me Ada (A-da).

I'm a tech girlie building an online brand that speaks to the modern woman with a diverse range of interests and experiences intersecting between the fields of art and technology.

I'm a software engineer by day, focused on building intuitive and accessible web experiences in the financial sector. So I talk a lot about web accessibility and inclusive design and how we can make the web more accessible for everyone.

Some of my favorite things to do are visiting cozy little coffee shops, walking through exhibits at museums / art galleries, and exploring new neighborhoods; locally and otherwise.

I've been to 5+ Canadian cities so far and I'm looking forward to visiting more! Also looking forward to a lot more international trips!

Because of my background as a software engineer, a demographic that has been said to be "notoriously allergic to being marketed to and quick to spot anything that doesn't look right", I am attuned to the importance of authenticity in content creation. My goal is to create content that presents information in an authentic manner, resonates with the audience and reflects my values while reflecting each brand's unique voice.
